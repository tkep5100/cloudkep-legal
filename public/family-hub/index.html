CloudKep Family Hub

A private, self-hosted household dashboard that brings shared calendars, upcoming events, tasks, reminders, shopping lists, weather, news, and other family information into one place.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

About Family Hub

CloudKep Family Hub is designed for use by members of a single household. It is not offered as a public software service, does not provide public account registration, and runs on privately operated household infrastructure.

Google Calendar integration

Family Hub can connect to Google Calendar after a user explicitly authorizes access through Google OAuth.

When authorized, Family Hub can:

  • Display calendars and calendar events selected by the household.
  • Keep a local synchronized copy of relevant calendar event information for the Family Hub interface.
  • Create or edit calendar events when an authorized household member explicitly requests that action.
  • Create a Family Hub task from a selected calendar event.
  • Create a calendar event from a selected Family Hub task.

Family Hub does not use Google Calendar data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or sale to third parties.

Private household service

Access to the Family Hub application itself is restricted to the household's private network and approved remote-access methods. This public site provides application and policy information only; it does not provide access to the Family Hub application.

Data practices

Details about how Family Hub accesses, stores, and uses Google user data are available in the CloudKep Family Hub Privacy Policy. Use of Family Hub is also subject to the CloudKep Family Hub Terms of Use.

Questions about CloudKep Family Hub or its data practices may be sent to admin@cloudkep.com.
